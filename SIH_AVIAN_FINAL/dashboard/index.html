Problem statement · SIH26201

Student Innovation-There is a need to design drones and robots that can solve some of the pressing challenges of India such as handling medical emergencies, search and rescue operations, etc.

AICTE Robotics and DronesSoftware

Our response: bridges and viaducts are infrastructure whose failure becomes a rescue operation. AVIAN inspects them before that, and can check a damaged bridge before rescue convoys cross.

Autonomy

Share of attempted waypoints completed without a stuck event — higher is better. PyBullet Stage 1 stuck on 68 of 104 waypoints and Stage 2 on 67 of 150; the Gazebo runs reached every waypoint they attempted (1,161 of 1,162 planned in full pass 05: 1 had no legal route in the plan and was not attempted). The bars come from different simulators, plans and flight stacks, so this is a progression, not a controlled before/after.

  • 187,468 control ticks flown with the sensed-only safety layer; it limited speed on 210 and pushed away from a return on 396 of them
  • Brake test: 1.943 m/s stopped in 1.132 m (budget 1.445 m)
  • Coverage flown by component: abutments 100%, piers 96%, deck furniture 95%; lowest: truss 65.1%

PyBullet Stage 2 stuck events: 13 near the target (settle failures) and 54 in transit, where the airframe was displaced by metres, not centimetres (mission/AVIAN_collision_diagnosis_FINAL.md).

Evidence gallery

Six Blender zoom tiles with boxes drawn from the headline model’s actual output: true positives, a false positive and missed defects, shown together.

Real-photo crack test (Ozgenel 2018 Concrete Crack Images for Classification, CC BY 4.0): 500 crack and 500 clean photos, headline model at threshold 0.65. Detected 0 of 500 cracks and flagged 0 of 500 clean photos: precision 0.0, recall 0.0. The detector does not yet transfer to real photos.

Bolts (FASTENER) are not reliably detectable at the current camera resolution.

Gazebo detections are not accuracy evidence

The trained detector runs in the loop during the Gazebo flights (about 7.8 frames per second measured in the column flight), so the pipeline works end to end. But the Gazebo world is untextured SDF primitives and its defects are flat-coloured spheres, so what it detects there says nothing about finding real damage. Scored against all 192 ground-truth defects at the true camera pose:

  • Full pass 05: 0 of 87 boxes contained a usable defect (3.5–10 m, line of sight). 77 box centres lay on bank-vegetation props and 10 on the road surface. Recall on the waypoint snapshots: 0 of 444 defect sightings.
  • Column flight: 61 of 233 boxes contained a defect, all on 2 dark marker spheres; above confidence 0.90, 0 of 56. Snapshot recall 1.9%.

Detector accuracy evidence comes only from the textured Blender imagery: the benchmark above and the inspection film.

Scope and known limits

What the numbers above do and do not show.

  • Detection does not yet transfer to real photos: 0 of 500 real crack photos detected, precision and recall both 0.0.detection/AVIAN_real_photo_eval_v2_FINAL.json
  • Trained and tested on synthetic Blender renders. On the held-out mission tiles, unseen-defect recall is 1 of 3 for every model, too few defects to tell the models apart.mission/coverage_score_mission_v3.json
  • Bolts (FASTENER) are not detectable at the current camera resolution.detection/AVIAN_detector_report_FINAL.md
  • Coverage is 81.1%, not 100%: flown coverage runs from 100% of the abutments down to 65.1% of the steel truss.gazebo/mission_follower/results/full_pass_05/coverage_flown.json
  • 1 of 1,162 planned waypoints was not attempted: the plan found no legal route to it.gazebo/mission_follower/results/full_pass_05/mission_log.json
  • The live detector in the Gazebo runs is not evidence of detection accuracy: that world is untextured geometry with flat-coloured defect spheres, and in full pass 05 0 of 87 boxes contained a real defect. Detection accuracy is quoted only from the Blender benchmark and film.gazebo/mission_follower/detection_eval/fp05_precision_strict_3p5_10m_los.json
  • Cap top faces cannot be inspected (the deck is directly above them); cap end faces are limited by the 3.5 m planned clearance.mission/columns_plan_report.json
  • Inspection film: beats 3 and 5 have detector dropouts, so their boxes flicker; beat 8's staged camera is 2.30 m from a deck edge.scene/film/detection_log.json, scene/film/clearance_result.json
  • Simulation only: nothing here has been flight-tested on hardware.project scope
